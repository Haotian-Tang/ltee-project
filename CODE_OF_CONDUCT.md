# Code of conduct

Participation in this project's issues, pull requests and discussions is
governed by the [Contributor Covenant, version 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/).

Report conduct concerns privately to **haotian.tang@mbzuai.ac.ae**.
Reports will be handled confidentially, with attention to the safety of the
people involved. Haotian Tang is the project's contact for enforcement.

The linked Contributor Covenant is maintained by the Organization for Ethical
Source under CC BY-SA 4.0; its attribution and terms remain with that text.
